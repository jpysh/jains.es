# Daily runbook

All times Madrid (CEST). Working window is 06:00–20:00; this owns the first
four and a half hours and then gets out of the way.

---

## The day

| Time | Block | Who | Min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 05:00 | Overnight run fires. Scout reads `sources/SOURCES.md`, drafts the session plan, the starting code, five beats, three news items. Opens a pull request. | agent | — |
| 06:00 | **Decide.** Read the PR on your phone over coffee. Accept the angle, swap it, or paste in a paper you want to cover instead. This is a decision, not a review. | you | 15 |
| 06:15 | **Study.** Today's session from `CURRICULUM.md`. Ends with one runnable artefact. | you | 120 |
| 08:15 | **Record.** Vertical, phone, Apple wired mic. One take, five beats. Say "clip" at each beat boundary. Beat 4 is always "the part I am least sure about". | you | 25 |
| 08:40 | Post-production: silence strip, loudness normalise, transcribe, cut 2–3 sub-60s verticals at the "clip" markers, burn captions, generate four thumbnails. | agent | 15 |
| 08:55 | Pick a thumbnail. Skim the captions for mangled jargon and names — Whisper reliably mangles both, and a wrong caption is worse than none for an ESL reader. | you | 10 |

**Your time: ~4h20.** Of that, 120 minutes is learning and about 100 minutes is
production. The rest is decisions.

---

## Why upload by hand for 30 days

Videos uploaded through an unaudited YouTube API project are **forced private**,
and the audit is a manual review measured in weeks with a brand-new channel as
a weak applicant. Hand-uploading costs five minutes, builds the upload history
that makes the audit approvable later, and removes a build item from week one.
Apply for the audit at day 30 with real history behind you.

---

## Video format

| | Spec |
|---|---|
| Aspect | 9:16 vertical, always. Readers are on phones. |
| Long-form | 5–7 min. Five beats. Posted as a regular video — **vertical over 3 minutes gets no Shorts-feed distribution**, and that is an accepted trade, not an oversight. |
| Shorts | 2–3 clips, each **under 60 seconds**, cut at the "clip" markers. These carry the Shorts-feed distribution. |
| Captions | Burned in on the Shorts, `.srt` sidecar on the long-form. 4–6 words on screen, slow dwell — the fast-flicker style that performs for native speakers measurably hurts ESL comprehension. |
| Cuts | None. Every high-performing reference channel is a single static shot; all pace comes from overlays. |
| Frame | Three zones, locked: headline card + day ribbon on top, one artefact in the middle, you at the bottom. |

## When the session fails

Roughly once a week the learning will not close — the loss goes NaN, the
derivation does not work, the Kaggle session dies at hour nine. **This is a
format, not a failure.**

Record: *"Here is what I tried, here is exactly where it broke, here is what
I will try tomorrow."* It needs no consolidated understanding, it is the one
kind of humour that travels across this audience — concrete self-reported
error rather than self-deprecation — and showing the method beats asserting
the conclusion. Budget one a week and do not treat it as a missed day.

---

## The buffer

Bank **finished days**, not footage. A banked beat still needs
post-production, a thumbnail, a wiki page and a newsletter — it saves the
wrong 20 minutes.

On a good day, record a second complete session. Two finished days in the
queue is what carries you through flu, travel, or a bad week at the other
business. Also keep one pre-written **"I'm away this week"** post. A stated
break is forgiven; a silent disappearance is not.

## Weekly, Sunday, 45 minutes

- Read the week's zero-result site searches. That list is next week's topics,
  written in readers' own words.
- Read `/usage`. Watch the token cost of the nightly run for the first month.
- Prune `sources/SOURCES.md` — anything that produced nothing in 30 days.
- Update the `stage:` marker on any wiki page that has matured.
